        What is anatomy, and what is simulated?

        Region names and broad arrangement follow published fruit-fly anatomy. Shapes and positions are hand-built schematics, not reconstructed neuron meshes. This panel runs 384 illustrative spiking units with hand-chosen connections and parameters; it does not load the FlyWire connectome. Activity follows the game, rather than controlling the fly or reproducing experimental recordings.

        The 2024 adult female FlyWire brain reconstruction contains 139,255 neurons and 54.5 million synapses. Those are reference dataset counts, not the number simulated here. The VNC is outside that brain reconstruction. Region names and ontology IDs were verified using Virtual Fly Brain. The research notebook loads live VFB records on request; these do not replace the schematic shapes or drive simulated spikes. The activity mapping is stylized: real regions, plausibly matched functions, game-simplified signals. Real neurons have no poker circuit, and this does not literally simulate a fly studying for a PhD. The lateral horn primarily processes olfactory information; its decision-game association here is a creative analogy. “Press”, “lower” and “hold” are game labels, not identified biological cell classes.
