What is anatomy, and what is simulated?

Region names and broad arrangement follow published fruit-fly anatomy. Shapes and positions are hand-built schematics, not reconstructed neuron meshes. This panel runs 384 illustrative spiking units with hand-chosen connections and parameters; it does not load the FlyWire connectome. Activity follows the game, rather than controlling the fly or reproducing experimental recordings.

The 2024 adult female FlyWire brain reconstruction contains 139,255 neurons and 54.5 million synapses. Those are reference dataset counts, not the number simulated here. The VNC is outside that brain reconstruction. “Press”, “lower” and “hold” are game labels, not identified biological cell classes.

  • Dorkenwald et al., 2024 — whole-brain wiring diagram
  • Schlegel et al., 2024 — cell types and brain annotation
  • Hulse et al., 2021 — central complex and navigation
  • Namiki et al., 2018 — descending motor pathways

FROM SENSING TO MOVEMENT

A brain needs a body.

  1. Sensory inputVision enters the optic lobes; odor signals enter the antennal lobes.
  2. Brain circuitsCentral regions integrate signals. Mushroom bodies contribute to learning and memory.
  3. Descending pathwaysSignals travel through the neck toward motor circuits in the body.
  4. Ventral nerve cordLeg circuits combine descending input with sensory feedback to coordinate movement.

In this game, load and lifting phase drive the illustrative neural activity. The displayed network does not determine muscle force.
